Solace Bible App

Find clarity. Feel grounded.

Solace is a calm Bible companion designed to help you read scripture, search by mood or theme, save meaningful passages, revisit your history, listen to sleep sounds, find nearby churches, and enjoy simple Bible-inspired games.

About Solace

Solace was created independently by one person, originally for my wife, with the aim of making scripture feel calm, accessible, and personal.

The app does not currently require an account or login. Most app data is stored locally on your device.

Privacy Policy

Solace is designed to be simple, calming, and respectful of your privacy. The app does not currently require an account and does not sell personal data.

Solace may store limited information locally on your device so the app can function properly. This may include saved passages, hidden passages, reading history, bookmarks, game progress, sleep settings, and other app preferences.

If you use the Find a Church feature, Solace may request access to your location. This is used to show nearby church results. Solace does not use your location for tracking or advertising.

Some features may rely on Apple services or external websites, such as Apple Maps or church websites. When you open external services, their own privacy policies may apply.

Solace does not currently include third-party advertising and does not sell personal information to advertisers.

For privacy questions, support, or feedback, contact solacebibleapp@gmail.com .
